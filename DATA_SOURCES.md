# Data sources and processing

Links below were copied from the original project or written from memory and **could not be opened in the offline development environment**. Check each before citing it.

## Used by the application

### Terrain: HiRISE digital terrain model (supported input, not bundled)
- Product: `DTEEC_045994_1985_046060_1985_U01` (stereo pair ESP_045994_1985 / ESP_046060_1985)
- Mission / instrument: Mars Reconnaissance Orbiter, HiRISE
- Page: https://www.uahirise.org/dtm/dtm.php?ID=ESP_045994_1985
- Credit: NASA/JPL-Caltech/University of Arizona
- Format expected: PDS3 `.IMG` (32-bit float) plus detached `.LBL`; about 1 m/pixel
- Used for: elevation, slope, route geometry, sky-view factor, scale bar
- Processing: `public/js/dem-format.js` (browser) or `tools/pds_to_tile.py`. Read in strips, invalid pixels (label `MISSING_CONSTANT`, non-finite, below −1e30) excluded, block-averaged by an integer factor, blocks with fewer than half valid pixels become no-data. The tile records the source ID, projection parameters and these steps.
- Georeference: equirectangular parameters from the label. Pixel centres are taken as 1-based, so the corner position may be off by up to one native pixel. Unverified against the real label.
- Limits: local coverage only; planner resolution is coarser than the product; elevation datum is as declared by the product.
- Observation period, spatial coverage: read them from the product page; not recorded here.

### Radiation reference: Curiosity RAD
- Value: 0.64 mSv/day surface dose equivalent rate (galactic cosmic rays), Gale Crater
- Reference: Hassler et al. 2014, Science 343, 1244797, https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1244797 (number quoted from memory; re-check)
- Used for: baseline of the modeled radiation layer and route dose. Scaled by sky-view factor (1 − mean sin² of horizon elevation over 8 azimuths, 2 km search) and a scenario multiplier (1.0 or an arbitrary 1.5).
- Limit: not measured at Jezero; ignores atmosphere, ground albedo and solar particle events.

### Mars local time: Mars24 algorithm
- Allison and McEwen 2000; NASA GISS tool https://www.giss.nasa.gov/tools/mars24/
- Used for: the "computed time at Jezero now" button (longitude 77.4° E). The small TT−UT term is omitted.

## Not integrated
Perseverance MEDA (https://pds-geosciences.wustl.edu/missions/mars2020/meda.htm), MOLA, CTX, CRISM, THEMIS, GRS, REMS. No file from these is read. Temperature, pressure and dust opacity are scenario formulas (`public/js/models.js`), not fits to MEDA.

## Synthetic data (not NASA)
`public/data/test-terrain.json` and `test/fixtures/*` are generated by `tools/make_test_dem.py` and `tools/pds_to_tile.py`. They contain no NASA data and exist for testing and demonstration.
